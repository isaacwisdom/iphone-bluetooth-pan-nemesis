iPhone Bluetooth PAN for NEMESIS 1.16

Copy all contents of this archive to the root of a USB drive and insert it after
the head unit has started. Confirm installation in NEMESIS Manager.

Open NEMESIS Manager > My Packages > iPhone Bluetooth PAN > Launch. Enable
Personal Hotspot on the paired iPhone, select it, and press Connect.

Automatic reconnection is off by default. This package is independent of HMG-API.
